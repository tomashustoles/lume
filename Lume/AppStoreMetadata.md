## Description

Transform your museum visits with Museum Companion. Point your camera at any painting and instantly discover its story, artist, and cultural significance.

**INSTANT RECOGNITION**
Our advanced AI recognizes artworks in seconds, providing detailed information about the painting, artist, period, and cultural context.

**TWO WAYS TO EXPERIENCE ART**

Info Mode: Get factual details about the artwork's history, technique, and significance.

Story Mode: Experience emotional narratives that bring the art to life and help you connect with the artist's vision.

**BUILD YOUR COLLECTION**
Save your favorite discoveries and build a personal art collection. Search, favorite, and revisit artworks anytime.

**BEAUTIFUL DESIGN**
Museum Companion features a minimal, elegant design inspired by museum aesthetics. Clean typography, generous whitespace, and thoughtful interactions create a premium experience.

**SYNC ACROSS DEVICES**
Your collection syncs seamlessly across all your devices via iCloud.

**FREE & PRO**

Free: 3 scans per day (resets daily)
Pro: Unlimited scans, unlimited collection

Perfect for:
• Art enthusiasts exploring museums
• Tourists visiting galleries
• Students studying art history
• Anyone curious about the artworks around them

Start your artistic journey today with Museum Companion.

## Review Notes

**Demo Account**: Not applicable

**Notes for Reviewer**:

Thank you for reviewing Museum Companion. Here's what you need to know:

**CAMERA TESTING**
The app requires camera access to scan artworks. You can test with:
1. Any painting/artwork photo on another device
2. Printed images of famous paintings
3. Museum postcards or art books

**FREE TIER LIMITS**
- Free users get 3 scans per day
- Resets at midnight local time
- To test Pro features, use the StoreKit testing environment

**API TESTING**
The app uses Gemini API for recognition. The API key is configured and will work in production. Response time is typically 3-5 seconds.

**SAMPLE ARTWORKS TO TEST**
- The Starry Night by Van Gogh
- Mona Lisa by Leonardo da Vinci
- The Great Wave by Hokusai
- Any classical or modern painting

**STOREKIT TESTING**
Use the included Configuration.storekit file for testing subscriptions in sandbox.

**PRIVACY**
- Camera access is requested on first scan
- iCloud sync is automatic if user is signed in
- No tracking or analytics

**CLOUDKIT**
The app uses CloudKit for syncing. It will work with any Apple ID signed into iCloud.

If you have any questions, please contact me@tomashustoles.cz
